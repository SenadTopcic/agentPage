## General Guidelines for All Images:

- **Style**: Professional corporate photography, high quality, cinematic

- **People**: If included, diverse, professional, engaged (but not the main focus)
- **Quality**: High resolution, sharp focus, professional color grading
- **Mood**: Professional, trustworthy, modern, welcoming
- **Avoid**: Stock photo clichés, overly posed people, dated aesthetics, heavy text overlays
